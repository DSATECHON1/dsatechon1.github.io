Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ON1Ecosystem may collect, use, store and protect information associated with visitors, members, website usage, advertising and participation in the ecosystem.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. The ecosystem is being developed to bring multiple digital services, participation features and opportunities together within one connected platform.

This Privacy Policy explains the general approach used when information is handled through the ON1Ecosystem website, member account features and related services.

By using the website or creating an account, you should review this Privacy Policy and the applicable Terms of Service so that you understand how the platform operates.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ON1Ecosystem may process different categories of information.

Account Information

When an account is created, information provided during registration may be used to establish and maintain the member account.

Participation Information

The platform may record information associated with participation, including ON1Coin activity, ON1XP activity, mining cycles, streaks, referrals and other eligible ecosystem activities.

Technical Information

Depending on the services and technologies in use, technical information may include browser type, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from technical data, pages visited and general website interaction information.

Information You Provide Voluntarily

Information may also be received when you contact the team, submit a partnership enquiry, request support or otherwise voluntarily provide information through an available website feature.

Please do not submit passwords, private keys, payment card details or other highly sensitive information through public contact forms or ordinary communications.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Creating and maintaining member accounts.
  • Authenticating members and providing access to account features.
  • Maintaining account balances, activity records and participation information.
  • Operating mining, streak, referral and ON1XP-related features.
  • Improving website functionality, usability and reliability.
  • Maintaining platform security and detecting suspicious activity.
  • Responding to questions, support requests and partnership enquiries.
  • Understanding general website usage and improving available services.

4. Member Accounts

If you create an ON1Ecosystem account, information associated with the account may be stored so that authentication, participation and member features can operate correctly.

Members are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and should take reasonable steps to protect access to their accounts.

If you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization, you should contact the appropriate ON1Ecosystem support channel as soon as reasonably possible.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ON1Ecosystem and third-party services used by the website may use cookies, local storage, pixels or similar technologies.

These technologies may support functions such as remembering preferences, maintaining website functionality, understanding general usage patterns, improving services and displaying or measuring advertising.

The technologies used may change as the ecosystem develops and additional services are introduced.

6. Advertising

ON1Ecosystem may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services. Advertising providers may use cookies, device information or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

The information collected by an advertising provider is generally subject to that provider's own privacy policy and advertising choices.

Users should review the privacy information of the relevant advertising provider for details about its data practices, cookies and available advertising controls.

Advertising services may change over time as ON1Ecosystem develops its platform and monetization arrangements.

7. Third-Party Services

ON1Ecosystem may rely on third-party services to support specific website functions. These services may include authentication, database infrastructure, hosting, advertising, analytics, security and other technical services.

Where third-party services process information, their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies, terms and applicable legal or contractual obligations.

Third-party services and providers may change as the ecosystem develops.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information handled through the ecosystem.

However, no website, internet connection or online storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore take reasonable precautions when creating accounts and accessing online services.

Members should use strong passwords, avoid sharing account credentials and sign out of accounts when using shared devices.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain account records, operate participation features, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, maintain security or satisfy applicable legal and operational requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected and the applicable requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

ON1Ecosystem does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact the team so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be considered.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, users may have rights concerning their personal information. These may include requesting access to, correction of or deletion of certain information.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification and to applicable legal, security or operational requirements.

Some information may need to be retained where necessary to maintain security, prevent abuse, resolve disputes or comply with applicable obligations.

12. External Websites and Links

The ON1Ecosystem website may contain links to third-party websites, services or platforms.

ON1Ecosystem does not control the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. Once you leave the ON1Ecosystem website, the policies of the external service apply.

You should review the privacy policy and terms of any external service before providing personal information or using that service.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ON1Ecosystem develops new features, changes service providers, introduces additional services or responds to legal, technical and operational requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised update date.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically so they remain aware of the current privacy practices described here.

14. Privacy Questions and Contact

If you have a privacy-related question, concern or request concerning ON1Ecosystem, you may contact the team through the available contact or partnership channel on the website.

ABOUT ON1ECOSYSTEM

A connected environment for digital participation.

ON1Ecosystem brings different digital experiences under one ecosystem. The project is being developed progressively, with currently available member features forming the foundation for additional services and partnerships.

What ON1Ecosystem is

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative focused on connecting services, participation, digital rewards, community activities and future digital opportunities through a common member experience.

Instead of treating every service as a separate destination, the ecosystem is designed so that members can access supported experiences through one connected account environment.

How the ecosystem develops

ON1Ecosystem is being developed in stages. Some member features are available while other services remain under development. Planned features are clearly identified so members can distinguish current functionality from future possibilities.

🔗
Keep services connected Member identity and ecosystem participation can work across supported services.
🛡️
Develop responsibly Security, transparency and sustainable development remain important project considerations.

ON1COIN

A digital component of ON1Ecosystem.

ON1Coin is the ecosystem's digital coin designed to support participation and future utility across supported ON1Ecosystem services.

Members can earn ON1Coin through eligible ecosystem activities where rewards are available. The exact availability and rules of each activity are controlled by the ecosystem's current reward system.

ON1Coin is currently used within the ecosystem participation experience. Planned services and future utility are subject to development and availability. ON1Coin should not be understood as a promise of cash value or guaranteed exchangeability.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about ON1Ecosystem.

A quick overview of the project's current functionality and development direction.

What is ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is a connected digital ecosystem initiative bringing member participation, ON1Coin, ON1XP, referrals, partnerships and planned digital services together.

What is ON1Coin?

ON1Coin is the ecosystem's digital coin designed to support eligible participation and future utility within supported ON1Ecosystem services.

What is ON1XP?

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress within supported ecosystem activities.

How does mining work?

Eligible members can participate in the available mining experience through the member dashboard. Mining rewards are subject to the current ecosystem rules and eligibility requirements.

What is the mining streak?

The streak system rewards consistent participation. The current planned structure progresses through a maximum Day 10 level, subject to maintaining the required participation schedule.

Can ON1Coin currently be exchanged for cash?

ON1Coin should not currently be treated as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Future utility and supported services depend on development and availability.

Are all ecosystem services available now?

No. Some member features are available while services such as ON1Pay, ON1Marketplace and ON1Exchange remain planned for future development.

How can I become a member?

You can create an ON1Ecosystem account through the registration page and then access the available member experience.

Can businesses partner with ON1Ecosystem?

Yes. Businesses, creators, service providers and organizations can explore partnership opportunities through the partnership section.

Where can I see my balances?

Members can view their ON1Coin and ON1XP balances through the member dashboard and wallet.

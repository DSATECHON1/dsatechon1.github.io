Understanding ON1Coin

A practical explanation of ON1Coin's current role within ON1Ecosystem, how member participation works, how mining and streaks are structured, and how ON1Coin differs from ON1XP.

Start Here

What is ON1Coin?

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. Its current role is connected to eligible member participation and reward activities provided through the platform.

ON1Coin is recorded as a separate balance in a member's wallet. It is different from ON1XP, which represents experience and participation progress.

The current ON1Coin experience is built around participation rather than a promise of guaranteed monetary value. The platform does not present the current ON1Coin balance as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset.

Important: ON1Coin's current role should be understood from the features that are actually available on ON1Ecosystem. Planned services and future utility should not be treated as current functionality.
How It Works

The current ON1Coin participation cycle.

Eligible members can participate through the member dashboard. The exact availability of an activity is controlled by the current platform implementation and its applicable rules.

1

Create and access your member account

Register for an account, sign in and access the member dashboard. Your account provides the environment in which supported ON1Coin participation is recorded.

2

Participate in the available mining cycle

Eligible members can start the available ON1Coin mining cycle from the dashboard when the required participation interval has been reached.

3

Receive the applicable mining reward

When an eligible mining action is successfully processed, the applicable ON1Coin reward is added to the member balance according to the current system rules.

4

Continue participation

Members who continue participating according to the applicable schedule can progress through the available streak structure.

Mining Reward

Current base mining reward.

The current member mining system uses a base mining reward that is separate from the additional streak reward.

⛏️

Base Mining

The current base mining reward is 50 ON1Coin per eligible 24-hour mining cycle.

⏱️

Mining Interval

A member's next base mining action becomes available according to the current 24-hour mining cycle.

Important distinction: The 50 ON1Coin base mining reward is separate from the streak reward. A qualifying participation cycle can therefore involve the base mining reward and an applicable streak reward.
Streak Rewards

How the 10-day streak structure works.

The current streak system provides an additional reward according to the member's consecutive participation day.

Day 10 is the maximum streak day. The current design does not continue to Day 11. Once the 10-day level is reached, Day 10 remains the maximum streak reward level.
Streak Timing

Why consistent participation matters.

The streak system is separate from the ordinary mining balance. It is designed to recognize consecutive participation rather than simply the total number of mining actions completed over time.

Under the current system rules, a sufficiently long gap between eligible mining cycles can reset the mining streak. In particular, a gap of 48 hours or more resets the next mining streak to Day 1.

Members should therefore pay attention to the mining availability shown in their dashboard rather than assuming that a previous streak will continue indefinitely.

Referral Rewards

How referrals relate to ON1Coin.

Referral participation is separate from ordinary mining and has its own eligibility condition.

A member can invite another person through the referral system. However, creating an account through a referral does not by itself make the referral reward immediately payable.

Under the current system design, the referred member must complete three consecutive eligible mining cycles before the applicable referral reward is processed.

When the required condition is completed, the referring sponsor can receive the applicable referral reward and associated XP according to the implemented referral rules.

The current referral reward structure is designed to connect reward eligibility with actual participation rather than rewarding an invitation alone.
ON1Coin vs ON1XP

The two balances serve different purposes.

🪙

ON1Coin

The digital coin balance used within supported participation and reward activities such as eligible mining, streak and qualifying referral rewards.

⭐

ON1XP

The experience and participation balance used to represent member progress and activity within the ecosystem.

ON1Coin and ON1XP should not be treated as interchangeable balances. They are maintained separately and have different roles within the current member system.
Wallet

Where your ON1Coin balance is displayed.

The member wallet provides visibility into the member's current ON1Coin balance and keeps it separate from ON1XP.

The wallet is intended to make the distinction between the two balances clear. As additional transaction-record functionality is developed, the wallet experience can provide more detailed activity information.

Members should use the dashboard and wallet as the primary places to check their current account information rather than relying on screenshots, social media posts or unofficial third-party claims.

What ON1Coin Is Not

Understanding the current limitations.

ON1Ecosystem currently does not present ON1Coin as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset.

A displayed ON1Coin balance should therefore not automatically be interpreted as a promise that the balance can currently be withdrawn, exchanged for a specific amount of fiat currency or used for every future service described in the ecosystem roadmap.

Future services such as payment, marketplace or exchange functionality remain subject to development, testing, operational readiness and applicable requirements.

For clarity: Only functionality that has actually been implemented and made available should be considered part of the current ON1Coin experience. Roadmap descriptions are development plans, not guarantees of future availability or value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common ON1Coin questions.

Is ON1Coin the same as ON1XP?

No. ON1Coin is the digital coin component used within supported reward activities, while ON1XP represents experience and participation progress.

How often can I participate in mining?

The current base mining cycle uses a 24-hour interval. The dashboard determines when the next eligible mining action is available.

What happens after Day 10?

Day 10 is currently the maximum streak level. The system does not introduce a Day 11 reward level.

Can I receive a referral reward immediately after someone joins?

No. The current referral design requires the referred member to complete the applicable participation condition before the referral reward is processed.

Does an ON1Coin balance guarantee cash value?

No. The current website does not present ON1Coin as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Future utility depends on actual development and service availability.

Where can I check my balance?

Authenticated members can view their ON1Coin and ON1XP balances through the member dashboard and wallet.

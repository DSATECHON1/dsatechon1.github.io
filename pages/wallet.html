Your ON1Ecosystem balances

ON1Coin and ON1XP serve different purposes within the ecosystem. ON1Coin is the participation balance associated with activities such as mining, streaks and eligible referral rewards. ON1XP represents experience and participation progress used for levels and future ecosystem activities.

The wallet displays information associated with your authenticated member account. Available features and future utilities may change as ON1Ecosystem continues to develop.

Understanding Your Wallet

🪙 ON1Coin

ON1Coin is the ecosystem's participation balance. Current member activities include the ON1Coin mining system, daily streak rewards and eligible referral rewards. Future utility will depend on the services and features that are actually developed and made available.

🎯 ON1XP

ON1XP tracks experience and participation progress. XP contributes to member levels and is intended to support future participation-based activities within ON1Ecosystem.

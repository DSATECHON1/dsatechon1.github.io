Partnerships & Participation

Building Through Participation and Partnerships

Partnerships and member participation are important parts of the long-term ON1Ecosystem development approach. This page explains how these areas fit into the platform and what members and potential partners can expect as development continues.

Why Partnerships Matter

A broad digital ecosystem cannot be built around one service alone. Partnerships can provide access to products, services, technology, campaigns and other opportunities that may complement the ON1Ecosystem.

ON1Ecosystem therefore intends to work with suitable businesses, service providers, developers and other organizations where a partnership can provide useful value to the ecosystem.

Potential Partnership Areas

💻

Technology

Technology partners may contribute tools, infrastructure, integrations or technical services that support future ecosystem features.

🛍️

Commerce

Commerce-related partnerships may support future marketplace, digital product or service opportunities.

🎁

Digital Services

Selected digital services may be introduced through suitable providers as the ecosystem expands.

📣

Campaigns & Promotion

Approved campaigns and promotional activities may create additional ways for members to discover participating services.

🌍

Community Activities

Community-focused partnerships may support activities designed to encourage constructive participation.

🤝

Strategic Collaboration

Longer-term collaborations may help expand the range of services and opportunities available within the ecosystem.

Member Participation

Participation is another part of the ON1Ecosystem model. Current member features are designed around activities such as account participation, ON1Coin mining, daily streaks, ON1XP and referrals.

Future partner-supported activities may introduce additional participation opportunities. The exact conditions, eligibility requirements and rewards will depend on the individual activity.

Participation rewards are feature-specific. The existence of a planned activity does not mean that the activity is currently available or that a particular reward is guaranteed.

How a Partnership May Work

1

Partnership Discussion

A potential partner and ON1Ecosystem discuss the proposed service, campaign, technology or activity.

2

Evaluation

The proposed partnership can be reviewed for suitability, technical requirements, user relevance and operational considerations.

3

Integration or Campaign Planning

Where appropriate, the parties can determine how the partner's service or activity could fit into the ecosystem.

4

Member Availability

Approved activities or services may be introduced to members once the necessary requirements have been completed.

Examples of Future Activities

As the platform develops, partner-supported participation could include educational activities, product discovery, service information, community campaigns or other legitimate digital experiences.

Product or Service Discovery

Members may be able to learn about a participating product or service through an approved ecosystem activity.

Educational Participation

A partner could provide useful educational material or information related to its area of expertise.

Community Campaigns

Approved campaigns could provide members with opportunities to participate in community or ecosystem initiatives.

Digital Service Offers

Suitable partners may provide information about digital services that are relevant to ON1Ecosystem members.

Current Development Status

ON1Ecosystem is still developing. Partnership capabilities and partner-supported activities may therefore change as the platform grows.

Planned activities should not be interpreted as currently available services. Availability depends on development, partner agreements, technical implementation and other operational requirements.

For Potential Partners

Organizations interested in discussing a potential collaboration can contact the ON1Ecosystem team with information about their organization, service, proposed activity and the type of collaboration they would like to explore.

Partnership discussions do not automatically mean that a proposal will be accepted. Each opportunity can be evaluated according to its relevance and suitability for the ecosystem.

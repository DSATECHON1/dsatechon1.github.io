Member Guide

How ON1Ecosystem Works

A practical guide to the current member experience — from creating an account and accessing your dashboard to participating in ON1Coin, ON1XP, streaks, referrals and other available activities.

Getting Started

Start with your member account.

The member experience is built around one account environment. Once registered, you can access the features currently available to your account.

1

Create an account

Begin by creating an ON1Ecosystem member account. Provide the required registration information and keep your login credentials secure.

Create your account →

2

Set up your member profile

After registration, your account provides the foundation for accessing the member environment. Keep your account information accurate and protect your credentials.

3

Access your dashboard

The dashboard is the main member area for accessing currently available participation features, viewing progress and starting eligible activities.

Go to member login →

Participation

How the current participation system works.

Available activities operate according to their individual rules. Rewards and progress are processed according to the conditions attached to each activity.

4

Start ON1Coin Mining

Eligible members can use the dashboard to participate in the available ON1Coin mining experience. Mining rewards are processed according to the current mining rules implemented by the ecosystem.

5

Maintain your mining streak

Consistent participation can progress your mining streak. Streak rewards are separate from the base mining reward and depend on the applicable streak conditions being met.

6

Earn ON1XP through eligible activities

ON1XP is maintained separately from ON1Coin. Eligible participation can contribute to your XP progress according to the rules of the activity.

7

Participate in referrals

Members can invite eligible new participants through the referral system. Referral rewards are not simply based on an invitation; applicable participation requirements must be satisfied before a referral reward is processed.

8

Check your wallet

Your member wallet provides a separate view of ON1Coin and ON1XP balances and related activity. This separation helps members understand the different roles of the two balances.

View member wallet →

Balances

ON1Coin and ON1XP have different roles.

The two balances should not be treated as the same type of value.

🪙

ON1Coin

ON1Coin is the digital coin component currently used within the ecosystem's participation and reward experience. Eligible mining, streak and qualifying referral activity can contribute to a member's ON1Coin balance.

⭐

ON1XP

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance. It represents progress within eligible ecosystem activities and is maintained separately from ON1Coin.

Availability

Current features and future services.

ON1Ecosystem is being developed progressively. A feature described as planned is not the same as an active service.

CURRENT

Member foundation

Account registration, login, member dashboard, wallet, ON1Coin participation, ON1XP, mining streak and referral functionality form the current member foundation.

DEVELOPING

Activities and partnerships

Additional approved activities and partnership opportunities can be introduced as the ecosystem's operational network develops.

PLANNED

ON1Pay and ON1Marketplace

Payment-related and marketplace services remain planned. Their availability depends on future development, testing, operational readiness and applicable requirements.

FUTURE

Expanded ecosystem services

Additional digital services may be considered as technology, partnerships, community participation and operational capacity grow.

Member Guidance

A few things to remember.

Check the rules. Each activity can have its own participation and reward requirements.
Keep your account secure. Do not share your password or private account information with other people.
Understand the difference between balances. ON1Coin and ON1XP have separate roles within the current ecosystem.
Allow for progressive development. Some services described on the website may still be under development or planned for the future.
Do not treat future plans as current availability. Only features that have been implemented and made available should be considered active member services.

ABOUT ON1ECOSYSTEM

What ON1Ecosystem is.

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. The project is being developed around a connected member environment rather than a single standalone service.

The current purpose

The current platform gives members a place to create an account, access a dashboard, participate in the available ON1Coin system, build ON1XP progress, participate in referrals and review their balances through the member wallet.

The broader ecosystem direction includes additional digital services and partnership opportunities. These services are being developed progressively and are not described as active merely because they appear on the roadmap.

This distinction is important because ON1Ecosystem is still growing. Current features and future plans have different availability and should be understood separately.

Development principles

🔎 Clear information Explain current features and participation rules in a way members can understand.
🧩 Progressive development Introduce additional services as development, testing and operational readiness progress.
👥 Member participation Build the ecosystem around useful member experiences and eligible activities.
🔗 Connected services Develop multiple services under one broader ecosystem direction.

ON1COIN

How the current ON1Coin system works.

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. Its current role is within the ecosystem's participation and reward experience.

⛏️ Mining Participation

Eligible members can participate in ON1Coin mining through the member dashboard. The current system uses recurring participation cycles rather than continuous device-based cryptocurrency mining.

Base reward: 50 ON1Coin for an eligible 24-hour mining cycle.
Streak: A separate streak reward can be added when the applicable consecutive participation condition is met.
Maximum streak: Day 10 is the maximum level and carries a 100 ON1Coin streak bonus.

🔥 Streak Participation

The streak system provides an additional reward alongside the base mining reward. Consistent participation progresses through the current Day 1 to Day 10 structure.

Day 1: 5 ON1 streak bonus.
Day 2–9: The streak bonus increases progressively from 10 ON1 to 45 ON1.
Day 10: 100 ON1 streak bonus, making 150 ON1 total with the 50 ON1 base reward.
24-HOUR CYCLE The base mining reward is associated with the current eligible 24-hour participation cycle.
48-HOUR RESET A participation gap of 48 hours or more resets the next mining streak to Day 1.
DAY 10 MAXIMUM Day 10 remains the maximum streak level rather than progressing to a Day 11 reward.
ON1XP

A separate participation balance.

ON1XP and ON1Coin serve different purposes in the current ecosystem. Keeping them separate helps members understand their balances correctly.

What ON1XP represents

ON1XP is an experience and participation balance. It can represent progress generated through eligible ecosystem activities.

ON1XP should not be treated as the same balance as ON1Coin, and an ON1XP balance does not automatically represent cash value.

ON1Coin compared with ON1XP

🪙 ON1Coin Digital coin component connected to eligible mining, streak and qualifying referral rewards.
⭐ ON1XP Separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress.
📋 Separate records The two balances are maintained independently within the member environment.

FAQ

Questions about the current platform.

What is ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1 designed to bring member participation, digital services, partnerships and future opportunities together.

What is ON1Coin?

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. It is currently connected to eligible participation and reward activities.

What is ON1XP?

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress. It is maintained independently from ON1Coin.

How much is the current base mining reward?

The current base mining reward is 50 ON1Coin for an eligible 24-hour mining cycle. Applicable streak rewards can be added separately.

What happens when I reach Day 10?

Day 10 is the maximum current streak level. Its streak bonus is 100 ON1Coin, making the total 150 ON1Coin when combined with the 50 ON1Coin base mining reward.

When does a mining streak reset?

A participation gap of 48 hours or more resets the next mining streak to Day 1.

How does the referral reward work?

The current referral condition requires the referred member to complete three consecutive qualifying mining cycles. The applicable reward is 25 ON1Coin plus 250 ON1XP.

Are all services shown on the website available now?

No. Some features are current while services such as ON1Pay and ON1Marketplace remain planned or under development.

Is ON1Coin guaranteed to have cash value?

ON1Ecosystem does not present ON1Coin on this website as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Future utility depends on actual development and service availability.

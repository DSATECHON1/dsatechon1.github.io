ON1COIN

Understanding the current ON1Coin participation system.

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. Its current role is within the ecosystem's participation and reward experience. The current system uses recurring participation cycles rather than continuous device-based cryptocurrency mining.

ON1Coin Mining

Eligible members can participate through the member dashboard. The current base mining reward is associated with an eligible 24-hour cycle.

Base reward: 50 ON1Coin for an eligible 24-hour mining cycle.
Streak reward: A separate bonus can apply according to the member's consecutive participation level.
Day 10: The maximum streak level currently provides a 100 ON1Coin streak bonus.

Participation Conditions

The mining and streak system is based on eligible participation cycles. Members should review the current rules rather than assume that every cycle produces the same total reward.

24-hour cycle: The current base mining reward is associated with the eligible cycle.
48-hour gap: A gap of 48 hours or more resets the next mining streak to Day 1.
Day 10 maximum: The current streak structure does not progress to a Day 11 level.
BASE 50 ON1Coin is the current base reward for an eligible 24-hour mining cycle.
STREAK A separate streak bonus is added according to the applicable consecutive participation level.
MAXIMUM Day 10 provides a 100 ON1 streak bonus, making 150 ON1 total with the 50 ON1 base reward.

ON1XP

ON1XP is separate from ON1Coin.

The two balances have different roles in the current member environment. Keeping them separate helps members understand their account records correctly.

What ON1XP represents

ON1XP is an experience and participation balance. It can represent progress generated through eligible ecosystem activities.

ON1XP should not be treated as the same balance as ON1Coin. An ON1XP balance does not automatically represent cash value.

ON1Coin vs ON1XP

ON1Coin Digital coin component connected to eligible mining, streak and qualifying referral rewards.
ON1XP Separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress.
Separate records The two balances are maintained independently within the member environment.

FAQ

Questions about the current platform.

What is ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1 designed to bring member participation, digital services, partnerships and future opportunities together within one developing platform.

What is ON1Coin?

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. It is currently connected to eligible participation and reward activities.

What is ON1XP?

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress. It is maintained independently from ON1Coin.

How much is the current base mining reward?

The current base mining reward is 50 ON1Coin for an eligible 24-hour mining cycle. Applicable streak rewards can be added separately.

What are the current streak rewards?

The streak bonus currently progresses from 5 ON1 on Day 1 through 45 ON1 on Day 9, with a 100 ON1 bonus on Day 10. Day 10 is the maximum current streak level.

What happens after a 48-hour gap?

A participation gap of 48 hours or more resets the next mining streak to Day 1.

How does the referral reward work?

The current referral condition requires the referred member to complete three consecutive qualifying mining cycles. The applicable sponsor reward is 25 ON1Coin plus 250 ON1XP.

Are all services shown on the website available now?

No. Some features are current while services such as ON1Pay and ON1Marketplace remain planned or under development.

Is ON1Coin guaranteed to have cash value?

ON1Ecosystem does not present ON1Coin on this website as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Future utility depends on actual development and service availability.

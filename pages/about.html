ON1Ecosystem

About ON1Ecosystem

ON1Ecosystem is a developing digital ecosystem created by DSATECHON1. It is being built to bring multiple digital services, member participation, community activities and future opportunities together through one connected platform.

What Is ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is designed as a platform rather than a single-purpose application. The long-term concept is to provide different services and participation experiences that can operate within the same ecosystem.

Development is taking place progressively. This means that some features are currently available to members, while other services are still being designed, developed or planned for future stages.

ON1Ecosystem does not represent every planned service as already available. Features are introduced as they become ready and supported by the platform.

Our Purpose

The purpose of ON1Ecosystem is to create a connected digital environment where people can access useful platform features, participate in eligible activities, maintain their account information and discover future ecosystem services from one place.

The platform also provides a foundation for working with suitable partners and introducing additional services as the ecosystem develops.

Current Member Experience

The current platform focuses on the foundation of the ON1Ecosystem member experience. Depending on the feature and its current availability, members can use areas such as account access, the member dashboard, ON1Coin participation, ON1XP activity, streaks, referrals and the member wallet.

These features are intended to provide the basic structure needed for a broader ecosystem while additional services continue to be developed.

ON1Coin and ON1XP

🪙 ON1Coin ON1Coin is the digital participation and reward unit used within supported ON1Ecosystem activities. Its availability and use depend on the rules of the relevant feature.
⭐ ON1XP ON1XP represents participation experience within the ecosystem. XP can be associated with eligible activities and member participation.
🔥 Participation Streaks Eligible members can maintain participation streaks through supported daily activities. Streak rules and rewards are determined by the platform.
🤝 Referral Participation The referral system allows members to invite other people to participate. Applicable referral rewards are subject to the platform's eligibility and reward rules.

Planned Ecosystem Services

ON1Ecosystem is intended to expand beyond its current participation features. The following services are part of the broader development direction and may be introduced progressively.

💳 ON1Pay A planned digital payment environment for supported transactions and ecosystem services.
🛍️ ON1Marketplace A planned marketplace concept for connecting users with products, services and selected opportunities.
🔄 ON1Exchange A planned environment for supported digital assets and related ecosystem services.
🎁 Gift Card Services Planned digital gift card-related services intended to become part of the wider ecosystem.
🤝 Partnerships Partnership opportunities may help introduce additional services, activities and experiences to the ecosystem.
🌐 Future Services The ecosystem is designed to remain expandable as new ideas, technologies, services and suitable partnerships are evaluated.

How Participation Works

ON1Ecosystem uses a participation-based approach. Members can interact with eligible features through their account and dashboard.

Participation may include activities such as daily mining, maintaining a participation streak, earning ON1XP, using referral features and reviewing account information through the wallet.

Each activity has its own conditions. Rewards are not guaranteed simply because an account exists, and members should review the applicable rules before participating.

Development Approach

ON1Ecosystem is being developed step by step. The project is focused on establishing a reliable foundation before expanding into additional services.

This approach allows individual features to be tested, improved and introduced progressively rather than presenting unfinished services as fully operational.

Development priorities may change as technical requirements, user feedback, partnerships and operational considerations evolve.

Our Approach to Members

ON1Ecosystem aims to provide clear information about its available features and distinguish current functionality from services that are still being developed.

Members are encouraged to use the platform responsibly, protect their account information and review the applicable Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Partnerships and Opportunities

Partnerships are an important part of the long-term ecosystem concept. Suitable businesses, service providers, developers and other organizations may be considered for collaboration where their services or capabilities can contribute to the ecosystem.

Partnership opportunities can involve technology, digital services, community activities, commerce, advertising or other areas relevant to the platform.

Who Is Behind ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem was founded in 2026 as an initiative of DSATECHON1. The project is being developed as a progressively expanding digital ecosystem.

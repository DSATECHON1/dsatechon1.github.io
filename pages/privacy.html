Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

ON1Ecosystem respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the ON1Ecosystem website and services, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you create or use an ON1Ecosystem account, we may collect information necessary to provide the service, such as your email address, username, referral information, account activity, participation records, XP and ON1Coin balances, and other information associated with your account.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve ON1Ecosystem, provide account functionality, maintain participation and reward records, operate referral systems, provide security, prevent abuse, communicate important service information, and improve the user experience.

3. Authentication and Data Storage

ON1Ecosystem uses third-party technology and infrastructure providers to support account authentication and secure storage of application data. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ON1Ecosystem and third-party service providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, preferences, analytics, and advertising where applicable.

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with a consent message and choices relating to cookies, personalized advertising, and similar technologies.

5. Advertising

ON1Ecosystem may use third-party advertising services to display advertisements. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertising in accordance with their own policies and applicable requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

ON1Ecosystem may integrate with third-party services, platforms, advertising providers, payment providers, analytics services, and other technology providers. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms that apply to their processing of information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect account and application information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain account and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce applicable terms.

9. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise manage certain information.

10. Children's Privacy

ON1Ecosystem is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as ON1Ecosystem develops, adds services, or changes how information is processed. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices relating to ON1Ecosystem, please use the official contact method provided through the ON1Ecosystem service.

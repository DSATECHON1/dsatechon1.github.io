ABOUT ON1ECOSYSTEM

One Ecosystem.
Built Step by Step.

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. The project is being developed as a connected environment where member participation, digital reward features, useful services and future ecosystem opportunities can be brought together over time.

Current development status: ON1Ecosystem is an active, developing project. Some member features are currently available, while other services described on this website are planned and have not yet been launched.
OUR PURPOSE

Why ON1Ecosystem exists.

The project is built around the idea of bringing related digital experiences into one connected member environment instead of treating every service as a completely separate destination.

One connected digital ecosystem.

ON1Ecosystem is designed to provide a common environment for members to create an account, access available features, participate in eligible activities, maintain their ecosystem balances and follow the development of additional services.

The platform is being developed progressively. This means that the current website distinguishes between features that members can use today and services that remain under development. Planned features are not presented as completed products.

The broader direction is to create an ecosystem that can support digital participation, community activity, partnerships and practical online services as the project develops its technology, operations and partnerships.

DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES

How we approach the ecosystem.

The project is being developed around several practical principles intended to keep the member experience understandable as new features are added.

🔎

Clarity

Current features and planned services should be identified clearly so members can understand what is actually available.

🧩

Progressive Development

New components are introduced progressively rather than treating an unfinished roadmap as a completed product.

👥

Member Experience

The platform is designed around simple navigation, account access, participation and understandable information.

CURRENT FEATURES

What is currently part of the member experience.

The following components form the current foundation of ON1Ecosystem. Availability and eligibility can depend on the applicable system rules.

Member Accounts

AVAILABLE

Members can create accounts and use authenticated areas of the platform to access the available ecosystem experience.

Member Dashboard

AVAILABLE

The dashboard provides a central location for participation information, balances, mining activity, progress and links to other member features.

ON1Coin Mining

AVAILABLE

Eligible members can participate in the current ON1Coin mining cycle through the member dashboard. The current base mining reward is 50 ON1Coin per eligible 24-hour cycle, subject to the system rules.

Streak Participation

AVAILABLE

Consistent eligible mining participation can progress a member through the current streak structure. The streak system provides additional ON1Coin rewards according to the applicable day and timing rules.

Referral Program

AVAILABLE

Eligible referrals can qualify for a referral reward after the required participation condition has been completed. The current referral reward is 25 ON1Coin and 250 ON1XP for the qualifying referred member, subject to the system rules.

Wallet

AVAILABLE

The member wallet provides visibility into ON1Coin and ON1XP balances and related activity. The two balances are maintained separately because they serve different purposes within the ecosystem.

ON1COIN & ON1XP

Two separate components with different roles.

ON1Coin and ON1XP are both part of the current member experience, but they should not be treated as the same balance or as automatically interchangeable assets.

🪙

ON1Coin

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. Its current role is connected to eligible participation and reward activities provided through the platform.

Base mining: 50 ON1Coin per eligible 24-hour mining cycle.
Streak rewards: Additional rewards can apply according to the current streak structure.
Referral rewards: Qualifying referrals can receive applicable ON1Coin rewards.
⭐

ON1XP

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress within eligible ecosystem activities.

Separate balance: ON1XP is recorded independently from ON1Coin.
Participation: Eligible activities may contribute to XP.
Progress: XP provides a way to represent participation and member activity progress.
DEVELOPMENT MODEL

Built step by step.

ON1Ecosystem is being developed progressively. The development approach separates the current member foundation from future services that still require development, testing, partnerships or operational readiness.

01

Build the member foundation

Account registration, authentication, dashboard access, wallet functionality and core participation features establish the foundation of the ecosystem.

02

Improve participation

Mining, streaks, referrals and XP-related activities provide structured ways for eligible members to participate and track their progress.

03

Develop additional services

Future services can be developed and tested independently before being presented as available ecosystem products.

04

Expand through partnerships

Suitable businesses, creators, service providers and organizations may contribute technology, services, promotional opportunities or community initiatives as the ecosystem develops.

ABOUT FAQ

Questions about ON1Ecosystem.

These answers summarize the current project direction and distinguish the existing member experience from planned development.

Who operates ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. The project is being developed as a connected platform for member participation, digital services and future ecosystem opportunities.

What is the main purpose of ON1Ecosystem?

The purpose is to bring multiple digital experiences and participation opportunities into one connected ecosystem while allowing new services to be added progressively.

What can members currently do?

Eligible members can create an account, access the dashboard, participate in the current ON1Coin mining system, progress through applicable streak rewards, use the referral program and view ON1Coin and ON1XP through the member wallet.

What is ON1Coin?

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. Its current role is connected to eligible participation and reward activities within the platform.

What is ON1XP?

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance. It is used to represent member progress and is maintained independently from ON1Coin.

Are ON1Pay and ON1Marketplace available now?

No. They are planned services. Their appearance on the website describes development direction and does not mean that those services are currently available to members.

Does ON1Coin have guaranteed cash value?

No. ON1Ecosystem does not represent ON1Coin on this website as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Any future utility depends on actual development, service availability and applicable requirements.

Where can I learn more about ON1Coin?

The ON1Coin Guide provides more detailed information about the current mining cycle, streak structure, referral participation and the distinction between ON1Coin and ON1XP.

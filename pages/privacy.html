Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ON1Ecosystem handles information associated with member accounts, website usage, advertising and participation in the ecosystem.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. We are committed to handling member and visitor information responsibly and providing clear information about how data may be used.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the ON1Ecosystem website, member pages, account-related features and other services operated as part of the ecosystem.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website and its available features, ON1Ecosystem may process information such as:

  • Account and registration information provided when creating an account.
  • Login and authentication information necessary to maintain account access.
  • Member identifiers and account-related information associated with participation.
  • Information relating to ON1Coin, ON1XP, mining activity, streaks, referrals and other eligible ecosystem activities.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from technical data, IP address and website interaction information where applicable.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us or submitting a partnership request.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, private keys or other highly sensitive information through public contact forms or communications unless a feature specifically requires it through an appropriate secure process.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Creating and maintaining member accounts.
  • Authenticating members and providing access to account features.
  • Recording eligible participation, rewards and account activity.
  • Operating mining, streak, referral and XP-related functionality.
  • Improving website functionality, usability, reliability and security.
  • Responding to questions, support requests and partnership enquiries.
  • Detecting abuse, fraudulent activity, unauthorized access or attempts to manipulate ecosystem features.
  • Understanding general website usage and improving the services offered.

4. Member Accounts

If you create an ON1Ecosystem account, information associated with your account may be stored so that the member environment can function correctly.

Members are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and should notify the appropriate support channel if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website or third-party services used by the website may use cookies, local storage, pixels or similar technologies to provide functionality, remember preferences, measure usage or support advertising.

The technologies used can vary depending on the features and third-party services active on the website.

6. Advertising

ON1Ecosystem may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

Third-party advertising providers may collect information about visits to this and other websites in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Users should review the privacy and advertising information provided by the relevant advertising provider for details about how that provider handles data and advertising preferences.

7. Third-Party Services

ON1Ecosystem may use third-party technologies and service providers to support functions such as authentication, database services, analytics, advertising, hosting, security or other website operations.

These third parties may process information as necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies and contractual or legal obligations.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information handled through the ecosystem. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Members should use strong, unique passwords, avoid sharing login credentials and take reasonable precautions when accessing their accounts.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain account records, operate participation features, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, comply with applicable obligations or protect legitimate operational interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is being maintained.

10. Children's Privacy

ON1Ecosystem is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the service inappropriately, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, users may have rights relating to their personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or other appropriate handling of their information.

Some information may need to be retained where necessary for security, legal, operational or other legitimate purposes.

12. External Websites and Links

The ON1Ecosystem website may contain links to third-party websites, services or platforms. ON1Ecosystem does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

When you leave the ON1Ecosystem website, review the privacy policy and terms of the external service before providing information or using its services.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ON1Ecosystem develops new features, changes service providers, introduces additional services or responds to legal and operational requirements.

When material changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

14. Contact

If you have a privacy-related question, concern or request concerning ON1Ecosystem, you may contact the team through the available contact or partnership channels provided on the website.

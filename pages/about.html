About ON1Ecosystem

ON1Ecosystem is a developing digital platform designed to bring multiple services, participation opportunities, partnerships and community rewards together in one connected experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create an ecosystem where users can discover useful digital services, participate in activities, connect with partners and experience different ecosystem features from one platform.

The Ecosystem

ON1Ecosystem is being developed progressively. Different services may become available at different stages as development continues.

🪙 ON1Coin A digital reward and ecosystem participation unit being developed for supported ecosystem utilities.
💳 ON1Pay A planned digital payment environment for future supported transactions.
🛍️ ON1Marketplace A planned marketplace connecting users, products, services and opportunities.
🔄 ON1Exchange A planned environment for supported digital assets and ecosystem services.
🎁 Gift Card Services Planned services focused on digital gift card opportunities.
🤝 Partnerships Activities and opportunities created through approved ecosystem partners.

Community Participation

ON1Ecosystem is designed around participation. Users may be able to earn XP, maintain daily participation streaks, complete eligible activities and receive ecosystem rewards according to the rules of each feature.

Development

The platform is being developed progressively with a focus on building useful services, responsible participation and a sustainable digital ecosystem.

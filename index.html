ABOUT ON1ECOSYSTEM

One Ecosystem.
Built Step by Step.

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. It brings member participation, ON1Coin, ON1XP, referrals, partnerships and planned digital services together within one connected direction.

OUR PURPOSE

Why ON1Ecosystem exists.

ON1Ecosystem is being developed around the idea that different digital experiences can be connected instead of being treated as completely separate services.

A connected digital environment

The project is designed to provide a common environment where members can discover available services, participate in eligible activities, track their progress and interact with different parts of the ecosystem.

The ecosystem is being developed progressively. This means that some features are currently available while other services remain under development or planning.

The distinction between current and planned features is important. ON1Ecosystem does not represent every planned service as though it is already available.

What the project focuses on

🔗 Connected experiences Bring supported member experiences together through a common ecosystem and account environment.
👥 Member participation Provide ways for eligible members to participate in available ecosystem activities.
🪙 Digital rewards Use ON1Coin and ON1XP for their defined roles within the current participation system.
🚀 Progressive development Introduce additional services as technology, testing, operations and partnerships develop.
OUR APPROACH

Principles behind the ecosystem.

The development direction of ON1Ecosystem is based on several practical principles intended to make the project easier to understand and use.

🎯

Clarity

Members should be able to understand what a feature does, whether it is currently available and what requirements apply before participating.

🛡️

Responsible development

Features are introduced progressively with attention to account security, reward processing, operational requirements and sustainable development.

🌱

Long-term growth

The ecosystem is designed to expand over time rather than presenting every future idea as an immediately available service.

ECOSYSTEM STRUCTURE

How the different parts fit together.

ON1Ecosystem is not a single feature. It is a collection of connected experiences with different purposes.

Member layer

The member environment provides the account and dashboard experience through which eligible users can access available participation features.

Account Registration, login and member profile functionality.
Dashboard A central place to access available ecosystem participation features.
Wallet A place to view ON1Coin and ON1XP balances and related activity.

Participation layer

The participation system connects eligible activities with progress and rewards according to the rules currently implemented by the ecosystem.

ON1Coin The ecosystem's digital coin used for eligible reward activity.
ON1XP A separate progress and experience balance used for eligible participation.
Referrals A system for inviting eligible new participants and processing applicable rewards.

Service layer

Additional ecosystem services are intended to build on the member and participation foundation as development progresses.

ON1Pay Planned payment-related ecosystem services.
ON1Marketplace Planned products and services marketplace.
ON1Exchange A planned environment subject to future development and applicable requirements.

Partnership layer

Partnerships can help introduce additional services, activities and opportunities that complement the ecosystem's development.

Businesses Potential collaboration with businesses and service providers.
Creators Potential campaigns, content and community activities.
Organizations Potential projects and participation initiatives.

DIGITAL REWARDS & PROGRESS

Understanding ON1Coin and ON1XP.

The two balances have different roles and should not be treated as the same type of value.

🪙 ON1Coin

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. It is currently used within the ecosystem's participation and reward experience.

Mining: Eligible members can receive ON1Coin through the available mining system.
Streaks: Consistent participation can qualify for applicable streak rewards.
Referrals: Eligible referral activity can result in applicable ON1Coin rewards.
Future utility: Additional uses depend on future development and service availability.

⭐ ON1XP

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance. It represents progress rather than being presented as a replacement for ON1Coin.

Participation: Eligible activities can contribute to a member's XP progress.
Progress: XP can help represent a member's activity within the ecosystem.
Separation: ON1XP is maintained separately from ON1Coin.
Future experiences: Additional uses may be introduced as the ecosystem develops.
DEVELOPMENT MODEL

Why some services are marked as planned.

ON1Ecosystem is being developed in stages. A planned feature is not represented as an active service until the necessary development and operational work has been completed.

CURRENT

Member foundation

The account, dashboard, wallet, ON1Coin participation, ON1XP, streak and referral foundation provides the current base of the ecosystem.

DEVELOPING

Participation and partnerships

Additional approved activities and partnership opportunities can be introduced as the ecosystem develops its operational network.

PLANNED

ON1Pay and ON1Marketplace

Payment and marketplace experiences remain planned services. Their availability depends on future development, testing, operational readiness and applicable requirements.

FUTURE

Expanded ecosystem services

Additional digital services can be considered as the technology, community, partnerships and operational capacity of the ecosystem grow.

TRANSPARENCY

Current features are different from future plans.

ON1Ecosystem is an evolving project. References to planned, future or developing services describe the direction of the project and should not be interpreted as confirmation that those services are currently available.

Similarly, ON1Coin is not presented on this website as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Its current role is within the ecosystem's participation and reward experience. Any future utility, exchangeability or additional service integration depends on actual development and availability.

Member responsibility Members should review the applicable rules and requirements of each activity before participating. They should also protect their account credentials and avoid sharing private account information.

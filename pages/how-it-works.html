Member Guide

How ON1Ecosystem Works

ON1Ecosystem combines a member account, participation activities, ON1Coin, ON1XP, referrals and a member wallet in one connected digital environment. This guide explains how the current system works and separates active features from services still being developed.

01 — Getting Started

Start with your member account.

The current member experience begins with an account. Your account provides access to the dashboard and the participation features available to you.

1

Create an account

Register for an ON1Ecosystem member account using the required registration information. Keep your login credentials private and secure.

2

Sign in

After registration, sign in to access your member environment. The dashboard is the central area for the currently available participation features.

Go to member login →

3

Use the dashboard

Your dashboard brings together the activities and account information currently available to your member account, including ON1Coin participation, progress information and access to other member areas.

02 — ON1Coin Mining

How the mining cycle works.

ON1Coin mining is a participation feature within ON1Ecosystem. The current system uses a recurring participation cycle rather than continuous device-based cryptocurrency mining.

When a member is eligible for a new mining cycle, starting mining records the participation cycle and applies the current base mining reward and applicable streak reward according to the system rules.

Base reward 50 ON1Coin per eligible 24-hour mining cycle.
Cycle timing A new mining cycle becomes eligible according to the current 24-hour participation interval.
Streak reward A separate streak bonus can be added when the applicable consecutive-participation condition is met.
Separate balances ON1Coin rewards are recorded separately from ON1XP.
03 — Mining Streak

Consistent participation increases the streak level.

The mining streak provides an additional ON1Coin reward alongside the base mining reward. The streak reward increases through Day 10.

Streak reset condition: A participation gap of 48 hours or more resets the next mining streak to Day 1.
04 — ON1XP

ON1XP tracks participation separately.

ON1XP is different from ON1Coin. It is maintained as a separate experience and participation balance within the ecosystem.

🪙

ON1Coin

ON1Coin is the digital coin component currently used in the ecosystem's participation and reward experience. Mining, streak rewards and qualifying referral rewards can contribute to the member's ON1Coin balance.

⭐

ON1XP

ON1XP represents experience and participation progress. It is maintained independently from ON1Coin and should not be treated as the same balance or automatically converted into ON1Coin.

05 — Referrals

Referral rewards depend on qualifying participation.

The referral system is designed around successful participation, rather than rewarding an invitation immediately.

1

Share your referral

An existing member can invite a new participant using the referral mechanism provided by the ecosystem.

2

The new member participates

The referred member must complete the required qualifying mining participation. An invitation alone does not immediately create the referral reward.

3

Three consecutive qualifying cycles

Under the current referral rule, the referred member must reach three consecutive qualifying mining cycles before the referral reward is processed.

4

Referral reward is processed

When the qualifying condition is reached, the current referral reward is 25 ON1Coin + 250 ON1XP for the eligible referral relationship.

06 — Member Wallet

Keep track of your balances and activity.

The member wallet provides a dedicated view of your account balances and related activity. Separating ON1Coin and ON1XP helps make the different purposes of the two balances easier to understand.

💰

ON1Coin balance

Your wallet can display the ON1Coin balance accumulated through eligible ecosystem participation, including applicable mining, streak and referral rewards.

📊

ON1XP balance

Your ON1XP balance is maintained separately and represents your experience and participation progress within eligible activities.

View the member wallet →

07 — Important Information

Understand what the current system does — and does not mean.

ON1Coin is not presented as guaranteed cash value. The current ON1Coin system describes a digital coin component within ON1Ecosystem. Members should not interpret a displayed ON1Coin balance as a guaranteed cash equivalent.
ON1XP is separate from ON1Coin. ON1XP is an experience and participation balance and should not be assumed to have the same function as ON1Coin.
Participation rewards have conditions. Mining, streak and referral rewards depend on the applicable system rules and qualifying participation.
Future services are not current services. A service described as planned or under development should not be treated as already available to members.
Keep your account secure. Never share your password or other private account credentials with another person.
08 — Availability

Current features and future services.

ON1Ecosystem is being developed progressively. The distinction below helps members understand what currently forms part of the member experience and what remains under development.

CURRENT

Member foundation

Account registration, login, dashboard access, member wallet, ON1Coin participation, mining streak functionality, ON1XP and referral functionality form the current member foundation.

DEVELOPING

Additional activities

Additional participation opportunities and partnership activities may be introduced as the operational side of the ecosystem develops.

PLANNED

ON1Pay and ON1Marketplace

Payment-related and marketplace services are part of the broader development direction but are not presented here as fully operational member services.

FUTURE

Expanded ecosystem services

Additional digital services may be developed as technology, partnerships, member participation and operational capacity expand.

ABOUT ON1ECOSYSTEM

A connected digital direction.

ON1Ecosystem brings different digital experiences together around a common member environment. The project is being developed progressively rather than presenting future ideas as already available services.

What is ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1. Its purpose is to create a connected environment where members can access available features, participate in eligible activities, track progress and discover additional ecosystem services as they become available.

The current foundation includes member accounts, a dashboard, wallet functionality, ON1Coin, ON1XP, mining participation, streaks and referrals.

Other services such as payment, marketplace and exchange-related experiences are planned for future development and are not represented as currently available.

What the ecosystem focuses on

🔗 Connected experiences Different supported features can operate within one ecosystem direction.
👥 Member participation Members can explore eligible activities available through the platform.
🪙 Digital rewards ON1Coin and ON1XP have separate roles within the participation system.
🚀 Progressive development New services can be introduced as development, testing and partnerships progress.

ON1COIN

A digital participation and reward component.

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of ON1Ecosystem. Its current role is within the ecosystem's participation and reward experience. Future utility depends on actual development and service availability.

⛏️ ON1Coin Mining

Eligible members can participate in the available ON1Coin mining experience through the member dashboard according to the current system rules.

Daily participation: Members can participate according to the current mining cycle.
Streak rewards: Consistent participation can qualify for applicable streak rewards.
Referral rewards: Eligible referrals can receive applicable rewards when required conditions are completed.

⭐ ON1XP

ON1XP is maintained separately from ON1Coin. It represents participation and progress rather than being presented as a cash-equivalent balance.

Participation: Eligible activities may contribute to XP.
Progress: XP provides a way to track member activity.
Separate balance: ON1XP and ON1Coin are recorded independently.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

A few basic answers about the current ON1Ecosystem member experience and development direction.

What is ON1Ecosystem?

ON1Ecosystem is a digital ecosystem initiative by DSATECHON1 designed to bring multiple services, participation opportunities and ecosystem features together.

What is ON1Coin?

ON1Coin is the digital coin component of the ecosystem. It is currently used within eligible participation and reward activities according to the applicable system rules.

What is ON1XP?

ON1XP is a separate experience and participation balance used to represent member progress. It is maintained separately from ON1Coin.

Are all ON1Ecosystem services currently available?

No. Some features are currently available while services such as ON1Pay, ON1Marketplace and ON1Exchange remain planned or under development.

How can I become a member?

You can create an account through the registration page and then access the available member experience through the dashboard.

Is ON1Coin guaranteed to have cash value?

No. ON1Ecosystem does not present ON1Coin on this website as a guaranteed cash-equivalent asset. Future utility depends on actual development and availability.
